How the puzzle works

  • Tap two numbers that are equal, or two that add up to 10, and the pair clears.
  • Pairs can sit side by side, stacked, or corner to corner on a diagonal.
  • Cleared cells don't block a pair — matches reach straight through the gaps.
  • The grid reads like text: the end of one line pairs with the start of the next.
  • Clear a whole row and it disappears; clear the whole field to reach a new stage.
  • Out of pairs? The + button writes your remaining numbers back onto the board.

I lost my progress

Progress is stored on your device only, so it does not follow you to a new phone, and deleting the app removes it permanently. We have no copy on a server and cannot restore it.

Remove Ads and Restore Purchases

"Remove Ads" is a one-time, non-consumable purchase. If you have bought it and it is not showing — for example on a new device signed in to the same Apple ID — open Me → Restore Purchases to restore it.

How do I turn off ad tracking?

Open Me → Settings inside the app. "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" opts you out of the sale or sharing of your personal information where the law provides that right. On iOS you can also revoke tracking permission in the system Settings app, under Privacy & Security → Tracking.

How do I delete my data?

Me → Settings → Reset Progress removes your progress and stored preferences from the device. It cannot be undone.

Accessibility

The app is portrait, single-handed, and playable offline with no timer. If something is hard to read or reach, tell us — it is a bug worth fixing.
