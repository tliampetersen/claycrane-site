Ten Match — Delete Your Data

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Ten Match is published by Clay Crane Productions. This page explains exactly how to delete the data the game holds, what gets deleted, and what is kept.

Delete everything stored on your device

  1. Open Ten Match.
  2. Go to the Me tab and open Settings.
  3. Choose Reset Progress and confirm.

This immediately and permanently erases your progress, stage history, daily-challenge results, awards, preferences, and the random identifier described below. It cannot be undone, and we hold no backup that could restore it. Uninstalling the app has the same effect.

Delete the analytics data we hold

Ten Match has no accounts. The only data that leaves your device is gameplay events (game starts, completions, hint use) sent to our analytics provider, Amplitude, tagged with a random identifier generated on your device. It is not linked to your name, email, or any real-world identity.

To have that deleted, email thomas@claycraneproductions.com with the subject "Delete my data". Because the game has no accounts and never asks for your email, we cannot look you up from your message alone — so tell us roughly when you started playing and how far you had reached, and we will work with you to identify the right records before deleting them. We will confirm by reply, normally within 30 days.

If you would rather not exchange emails at all: using Reset Progress in the app erases the identifier that ties those events to your device, which leaves the remaining analytics permanently anonymous.

What is deleted, and what is kept

  • Deleted: all gameplay events and the random device identifier associated with them, plus everything stored locally on your device.
  • Kept: nothing that identifies you or your device. We may retain aggregate, anonymous counts which cannot be traced back to any individual and are therefore not deleted.
  • Retention: analytics events are retained for at most 24 months, after which they are removed automatically whether or not you ask.

Purchases

Ten Match offers one non-consumable purchase, "Remove Ads". Purchase records are held by Apple, not by us; we keep no payment information. You can restore the purchase with Me → Restore Purchases.
