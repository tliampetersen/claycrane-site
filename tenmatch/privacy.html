Ten Match — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Ten Match is a numbers puzzle by Clay Crane Productions. This policy describes what the app collects, why, and the choices you have.

No account, no sign-up

Ten Match has no accounts. We never ask for your name, email address, contacts, or location, and the app works fully offline.

Game progress

Your progress (stages, scores, daily challenges, awards, settings) is stored locally on your device. It is not uploaded to any server. Deleting the app — or using Me → Settings → Reset Progress inside the app — removes it.

Analytics

To understand how the game plays and improve it, Ten Match sends gameplay events (such as game starts, completions, and hint use) to Amplitude, our analytics provider. These events are tied to a random identifier generated on your device — not to your identity. They do not include precise location, contacts, or any content from your device. See Amplitude's privacy policy. To request deletion of analytics data for your device, email us at the address below.

In-app purchase

Ten Match offers one optional, non-consumable purchase, "Remove Ads". Purchases are processed by Apple, not by us; the transaction is handled through Apple's StoreKit and our purchase provider, RevenueCat, which receives the receipt to unlock the purchase on your device. We never see your payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Attribution

To measure which channels bring players to the app, Ten Match uses Tenjin, an attribution provider, which processes device identifiers to match an install to its source. See Tenjin's privacy policy.

Notifications

If you enable them, Ten Match schedules reminders locally on your device. There is no push server; nothing about notifications leaves your device. You can turn them off any time in the app's settings or your system settings.

Advertising

When advertising is active in the app, ads are served by AppLovin MAX and its mediation partners. Ad networks may process device information — such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, and coarse (city-level) location derived from it — to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. See AppLovin's privacy policy. On iOS the app asks for tracking permission before any such use; you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and the in-app "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" control opts you out of the sale or sharing of your information where the law provides that right.

Children

Ten Match is a general-audience game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app contains no chat, no social features, and no free-text input.

Changes

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated first, and the "Last updated" date revised.

Contact
