How a turn works

  • Build your squadron — pick pilots, ships and weapon loadouts from the cards.
  • Command in secret — both players lock in maneuvers behind their screens at the same time.
  • Reveal and fire — line up the shot, roll a fistful of dice, punch through shields and armor.

Elegant rules you learn in one game; enough pilot skills, weapons, terrain and mission objectives to stay fresh for hundreds of dogfights.
