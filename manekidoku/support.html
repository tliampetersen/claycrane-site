Something not working, or just want to tell us about a puzzle? Email thomas@claycraneproductions.com and we will get back to you.

How the puzzle works

  • The board is split into coloured regions. Exactly one cat belongs in each.
  • No two cats may share a row or a column, and cats will not sit next to each other — not even diagonally.
  • Single-tap a square to rule it out. Double-tap to place a cat.
  • Every board is solvable by logic alone. You never have to guess.

I lost my progress

Progress is stored on your device only, so it does not follow you to a new phone, and deleting the app removes it permanently. We have no copy on a server and cannot restore it.

A puzzle looks impossible

Every level is verified to have exactly one solution reachable by deduction. If you are stuck, a hint will show you which row, column, or colour forces the next square. If you believe a board is genuinely broken, email us the level number and we will check it.

How do I turn off ad tracking?

Open Settings inside the app. "Do Not Sell or Share My Info" opts you out of the sale or sharing of your personal information where the law provides that right. On iOS you can also revoke tracking permission in the system Settings app, under Privacy & Security → Tracking.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Delete My Data removes your progress and stored preferences from the device. It cannot be undone.

Accessibility

The board uses distinct colours chosen for contrast under the common forms of colour vision deficiency, and every placed cat is also marked by its shape rather than colour alone. If something is hard to read or reach, tell us — it is a bug worth fixing.
